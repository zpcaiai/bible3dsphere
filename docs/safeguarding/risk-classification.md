# MissionBridge Risk Classification

| Level | Examples | AI authority | Human path |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L0 | ordinary questions | provide general information | normal team workflow |
| L1 | low mood, family conflict, financial pressure | supportive suggestions and referral options | trained worker review when requested |
| L2 | worsening mental health, domestic abuse indicators, child-safety concern | recommend immediate escalation only | safeguarding lead and qualified partner review |
| L3 | explicit self/other harm, abuse, missing person, emergency medical danger | show emergency guidance and escalate; never close | emergency services/local professional response |

AI and ordinary workers cannot downgrade or close L2/L3 records. Information is disclosed only to the minimum authorized response team.
