# MissionBridge Incident Response

1. Confirm immediate danger without conducting a clinical investigation.
2. Create the minimum necessary incident record and classify L0-L3.
3. For L3, display local emergency guidance and involve a real person immediately.
4. For L2, notify the safeguarding lead and an appropriate professional partner.
5. Preserve the event and escalation timeline; never promise absolute confidentiality.
6. Restrict access, redact operational logs, and avoid unnecessary location or family data.
7. Only authorized safeguarding personnel may resolve L2/L3 after documenting external follow-up.
8. Review the response, retention deadline, and participant communication after stabilization.
