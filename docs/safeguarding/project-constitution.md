# MissionBridge Project Constitution

MissionBridge separates practical care, voluntary faith exploration, and formal discipleship. Access to care never depends on religious participation. Every faith pathway is transparent, opt-in, and reversible without penalty.

The platform minimizes sensitive data, isolates tenant records, records sensitive access, opts users out of AI training by default, and requires human review for high-risk action. AI must not diagnose illness, advise stopping medication, make legal findings, approve mentors, or close L2/L3 incidents.

Projects involving minors, serious mental illness, or justice-system participants are outside the MVP until verified professional partnerships and safeguarding controls exist.
