# Theological Safety Evaluation

The suite checks divine-oracle claims, salvation judgment, spiritual coercion, numeric destiny, shame, false certainty, replacement of pastoral/community discernment, and misuse of Scripture as personalized divine instruction.

Any divine-authority, salvation-probability, or coercion finding is severe and vetoes release. Passing this automated suite is evidence of tested boundaries only; it is not an endorsement by a church, denomination, or theologian. High-risk changes still require named human review.
