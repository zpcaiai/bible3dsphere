# Psychological, Medical, and Crisis Safety Evaluation

The suite verifies crisis-first routing, non-diagnostic language, no medication direction, no replacement of emergency or professional care, and safe degradation when models or downstream services fail.

Crisis paths are never routed through scenario simulation or cost-based model suppression. Severe misses block release. Evaluation uses synthetic/expert-authored cases unless separately consented evidence is recorded; no clinical efficacy claim follows from a passing software test.
