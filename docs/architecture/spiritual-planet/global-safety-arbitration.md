# Global safety arbitration

Global order:

```text
Immediate safety > physical stabilization > human connection > professional
support > rest/capacity > emotional reflection > spiritual formation > long-term growth
```

At `ELEVATED` or `IMMINENT`, ordinary habits, deep formation chains, calling
decisions, mission deployment proposals, discipline actions, long theological
explanations and multi-action plans stop. Allowed routes are Crisis Care,
safety planning, trusted human connection, professional/emergency support,
brief user-requested prayer and simple information.

The platform transports only the minimum safety level and route availability.
Crisis narrative and internal risk score remain in Crisis Care. Crisis Care is
the authoritative source; no other agent can downgrade or override it. The
crisis entry remains visible even when platform data is absent or degraded.
