# Recommendation arbitration

Priority order:

1. crisis and immediate safety;
2. medical or real-world safety;
3. current explicit user request;
4. user protection plan;
5. capacity and rest;
6. minimum alternative for a confirmed formation pattern;
7. ordinary prayer/formation/habit/attention proposal;
8. learning and long-term growth.

The deterministic arbitrator removes expired candidates, rejects candidates
driven by pending context, merges duplicate human-connection or matching
deduplication keys, applies the three-active-action limit, reduces medium/high
burden to at most two minutes in low capacity and exposes one action at most.

Suppression reasons remain available to developers and audit, while the user
sees a single coherent voice. No module priority contest, completion score,
streak, rank or moral interpretation is produced.
