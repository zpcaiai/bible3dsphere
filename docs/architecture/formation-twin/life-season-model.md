# Life Season Model

Life Seasons provide context for work, family, church, health, care, recovery,
relocation, rest, and calling transitions. They are controlled by the user.
`SPIRITUAL_DRYNESS_SELF_REPORTED` is accepted only as a user description; the
engine never announces a spiritual wilderness or divine purpose.

Each season has a title, type, approximate precision, start/end, life domains,
roles, source kind, and review status. User-created seasons are immediately
reviewed as user statements. A future model suggestion must remain pending until
the user confirms it.

Pattern-to-season links are explicit records. When a season closes, related
active patterns become contextual and due for review; they do not become global.
Deleting a season removes it from current context and marks its pending
candidates outdated while preserving source events.

The UI supports creation, editing, closure, reopening, and a limited closure
review. Titles may be exported to the owner but are excluded from event payloads,
metrics labels, and ordinary technical health data.
