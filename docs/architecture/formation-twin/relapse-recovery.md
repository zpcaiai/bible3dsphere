# Relapse Recovery

Recovery is separate from pattern analysis. The required sequence is immediate
safety, stopping continuation, human connection, one recovery action and an
optional later review. The first question is “Are you safe now?” rather than a
request to explain why the event occurred.

Ordinary user-reported relapse does not automatically become a crisis. Self-harm,
harm to others, violence, acute intoxication/medical danger or another Crisis
Care condition stops ordinary analysis and routes to the existing crisis system.
The crisis body is not copied into Formation Twin.

Deep review remains disabled until stabilization. A 24–72 hour review is
optional, limited to four questions and can be skipped. Cycle-update proposals
remain candidates until separately confirmed; one relapse never invalidates all
formation, grace, protection or recovery evidence.
