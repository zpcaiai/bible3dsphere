# Scenario Non-Prediction Policy

Every scenario displays this boundary: **这是基于你确认的假设生成的有限情景，不是预测、预言、诊断或神旨意判断。**

The system must never:

- assign a probability to relapse, salvation, spiritual gain, divine favor, or life outcomes;
- claim to know God's hidden will or speak with divine authority;
- present one branch as the inevitable or optimal future;
- issue medical, psychiatric, legal, financial, or crisis-care instructions;
- turn a scenario into an action without a separate user confirmation and safety review.

The backend enforces the same rule regardless of UI wording. Prohibited phrases and probability-like output are release-blocking evaluation failures. Uncertainty and counterevidence are required fields, not optional explanatory copy.
