# Theological Safety for Formation

## Non-negotiable boundaries

Generated content is blocked when it claims or implies:

- a person's salvation or regeneration status;
- whether repentance or confession is genuine;
- a direct oracle, divine command, or God's specific will;
- hidden motives or an inner truth the user does not know;
- an automatic idol, sin, or root-cause verdict;
- a psychological or personality diagnosis;
- spiritual, maturity, holiness, sin, idol, or salvation scores/ranks;
- deterministic spiritual causality.

The shared `TheologicalSafetyService` also checks prosperity-gospel blame, toxic positivity, magical prayer promises, AI replacement of pastors/church, shame, and suffering-as-certain-sin language.

## Crisis-first rule

Concern/elevated/imminent safety metadata and `ROUTED_TO_CRISIS` records never enter formation processing. Before optional text inference, the decrypted authorized text is scanned again. High or critical risk stops inference and routes responsibility to the existing crisis-care layer; no formation interpretation is generated.

## Evidence language

Safe wording identifies source and uncertainty: “you recorded,” “observed in the same event,” “possibly related, awaiting your review,” or “you confirmed.” Unsafe wording uses “proves,” “must mean,” “your real motive,” or “God told the system.”

## Human and church boundary

Formation Twin can help a user organize their own record and formulate questions. It does not act as pastor, therapist, spiritual director, accountability partner, or church authority. It does not auto-send conclusions to any person or module.

## Red-team gate

Unit tests cover salvation, repentance, oracle, hidden motive, idol/sin verdict, diagnosis, personality, ranking, and absolute-cause attacks. The database independently rejects forbidden causal relation codes and user reports with system confidence.
