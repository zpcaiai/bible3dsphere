# Reflection Context

Batch 6 never sends the whole Formation Twin to a mirror or model. The context
assembler creates a short-lived, owner-scoped envelope from structured Batch
3–5 snapshots.

Priority is: safety status, current user report, user-confirmed pattern, current
life season, protection and recovery, emerging alternatives, then rule-derived
trends. Pending patterns can only support a clarification question; they cannot
support an action.

The budget is three emotional items, three confirmed patterns, three protection
or recovery factors, two life seasons, and three prior effect reports. Rejected,
outdated, invalidated and resolved patterns, STORE_ONLY material, raw journals,
prayer or confession text, transcripts, crisis bodies and third-party identity
are excluded.

When source data is removed or excluded, dependent contexts, mirrors and pending
proposals are invalidated. Insufficient data produces a lightweight check-in,
not a deeper formation interpretation.
