# Non-Shaming Language

The system describes a temporary context and an available protection, never a
fixed identity. Recommended language distinguishes “you reported an urge,” “a
behavior started” and “you reported the old behavior occurred.” It says risk is
not destiny and that an event does not define the whole person.

Prohibited language predicts sin/relapse, assigns addiction or spiritual
identity, claims divine punishment/revelation, questions genuine repentance,
requires disclosure to a pastor, treats rejection as disobedience, or demands
more monitoring as proof of sincerity.

Recovery copy prioritizes stopping further impact and reconnecting with support.
Confession and prayer may be user-chosen, but cannot replace environment change,
medical help, emergency support or human connection. Feedback optimizes lower
burden and fewer false positives—not compliance.
