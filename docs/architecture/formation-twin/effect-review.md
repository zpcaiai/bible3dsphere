# Intervention Effect Review

Execution and effect are separate. Completed means the action was recorded as
done; it does not mean a formation pattern changed.

The optional review asks at most whether it was done, whether it helped, how
burdensome it was, and what should change next time. All effects are stored as
USER_REPORTED_FACT. NOT_STARTED, STOPPED, FORGOTTEN and DECLINED are neutral
execution states.

A single helpful report cannot establish causality. Repeated user reports in a
similar context may become a future protection candidate, still with uncertainty
and counterevidence. High burden shortens future suggestions; an unhelpful type
may be deprioritized.

Preference learning is user-scoped, visible, resettable and independently
disableable. It is not shared-model training and cannot lower safety thresholds.
