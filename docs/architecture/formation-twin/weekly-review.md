# Weekly Formation Review

Weekly review is optional and available at most once per week through the
existing scheduler or an explicit user request.

It contains no more than three important observations, one focus theme, one
question and one optional action. It separates burden factors from grace,
protection, recovery and emerging alternatives. The review always states how
many days were actively recorded and that missing days may contain unobserved
experience.

Comparisons are scoped to recorded data. “Recorded stress was higher” is
permitted; “you were generally more anxious” requires the user's confirmation.
Completion count is not a formation outcome.

Complete, skip and correct are neutral lifecycle actions. Correction creates a
new version; skip does not create a negative label or an automatic reminder.
