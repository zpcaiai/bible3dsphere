# Anti-Gamification and AI Dependency

The reflection surface has no streak counter, spiritual points, growth level,
badge, rank, church leaderboard, red failure progress bar, obedience score or
compliance metric. Completion frequency is not a proxy for growth.

Neutral history, user-selected celebrations, gratitude, a new response and a
weakening old pattern are permitted. Users can pause every intervention feature
without penalty.

The system does not optimize acceptance, task completion or time in product.
Repeated rejection, skipping or high burden lowers reminder frequency rather
than being interpreted as resistance.

When product behavior suggests the system is being asked to replace real
discernment or relationships, the boundary message recommends a trusted person,
local church or professional support without diagnosing dependency or notifying
anyone automatically.
