# Micro-Intervention Library

The versioned library covers pause, rest, body regulation, emotion naming,
short reflection, honest prayer, Scripture reflection, relational support,
attention boundaries, reconciliation preparation, professional support,
short-term habits, crisis handoff and NO_ACTION.

Every template declares duration, effort, capacity and safety compatibility,
target module, contraindications and theological safety notes. Rest and human
support are first-class support, not failures to spiritualize a problem.

Confession requires a user-confirmed behavior. Lament must not be bypassed by a
forced gratitude task. Prayer and Scripture do not replace medical, mental
health, legal or practical help. Relationship support produces a draft or
reminder and never sends a message automatically.

NO_ACTION means “keep the insight without adding a practice” and remains
available in every ordinary flow.
