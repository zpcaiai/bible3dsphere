# Crisis vs Relapse Routing

Temptation, urge, behavior and user-reported relapse are not synonymous with a
crisis. Ordinary recovery may use environment change, a support draft, a short
pause, prayer or professional-support preparation without emergency escalation.

Crisis Care becomes authoritative for self-harm, harm to others, violence,
acute overdose/intoxication, severe withdrawal or medical danger, inability to
maintain basic safety, abuse risk or another Crisis Care determination. The
order is safety, physical stabilization, human/professional connection, then
meaning and formation.

During crisis routing, ordinary warnings, long reflection, theological
interpretation and multi-step tasks stop. The Formation Twin stores only a
minimal route reference; it does not copy crisis narrative into snapshots,
events, notifications, graphs or model logs.
