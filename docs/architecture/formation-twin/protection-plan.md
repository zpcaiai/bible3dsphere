# Protection Plan

A protection plan is prepared during a stable period and is private by default.
It records early signs, high-risk contexts, one preferred protection action, up
to three alternatives, environment boundaries, support references, escalation
rules and optional spiritual/professional supports.

Activation requires user confirmation. Plans can be rehearsed without executing
an external action, paused, versioned and deleted. Environment boundaries default
to `REMINDER_ONLY`; hard block and accountability unlock require a separate
current choice, visible recovery method and safe access to emergency/medical
tools.

Prayer and spiritual practices remain optional supports, never prerequisites for
leaving an environment, contacting a person or seeking professional care. A plan
must not encode “days without relapse,” purity streaks or moral scores.
