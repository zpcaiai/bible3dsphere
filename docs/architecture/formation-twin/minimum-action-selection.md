# Minimum Action Selection

At most three candidates are built internally. The user sees one and may ask for
another category or a smaller step.

Hard constraints exclude crisis conflicts, a blocked category, actions beyond
current capacity, unconfirmed sources, unauthorized data, medical or legal risk,
third-party disclosure, dangerous immediate relationship conversations and
religiously compulsive actions. Safety and refusal override ranking.

The smaller-step ladder is 10 minutes, 3 minutes, 1 minute, record only, then
NO_ACTION. Very-low capacity permits at most one minute. High capacity still
does not cause multiple actions to be stacked.

Any ranking utility is internal and cannot be exposed, compared between users,
or optimized for streaks, completion or engagement time.
